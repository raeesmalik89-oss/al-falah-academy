🎓 Meet Our Founder & CEO

Learn One-on-One with Our Founder, Dr. Muhammad Naeem

Founder & CEO of Al-Falah Islamic Academy · PhD in Tafseer & Quranic Sciences (IIUI) · Jamia Ashrafia graduate · Ustad-ul-Tafseer & Hadith at Darul Uloom Farooqia, Rawalpindi

⭐ Featured Scholar

Dr. Muhammad Naeem

Founder & CEO · Tafseer, Hadith & Islamic Studies
Jamia Ashrafia Graduate PhD – Tafseer & Quranic Sciences Ustad-ul-Tafseer & Hadith – Jamia Farooqia Visiting Faculty – IIUI Visiting Faculty – FAST-NUCES Visiting Faculty – FJWU

Dr. Muhammad Naeem is one of our most distinguished scholars. He completed his foundational Islamic education from the prestigious Jamia Ashrafia, Lahore — one of Pakistan's most renowned Darul Ulooms. He then went on to earn his PhD in Tafseer & Quranic Sciences from the International Islamic University Islamabad (IIUI). He currently serves as Ustad-ul-Tafseer & Hadith at Darul Uloom Farooqia (Gulzar Jamia Masjid), Qaid-e-Azam Colony, Rawalpindi, and as a Visiting Faculty Member at the International Islamic University Islamabad (IIUI), FAST-NUCES University, Islamabad and Fatima Jinnah Women University, Rawalpindi, where he teaches Tafseer, Hadith, Islamic Studies and Research Methodology. Dr. Naeem specializes in Tafseer and Quranic sciences, Hadith, and Islamic jurisprudence (Fiqh).
