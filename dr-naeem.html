About the Scholar

A Life Devoted to the Quran & Sunnah

Bridging classical Islamic scholarship with contemporary education.

Dr. Muhammad Naeem is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Al-Falah Islamic Academy, where he leads the institution's academic vision, scholarly standards, and strategic development. With a PhD in Tafseer & Quranic Sciences from the International Islamic University Islamabad (IIUI), alongside classical Islamic training at Jamia Ashrafia, Lahore, he brings together the depth of traditional scholarship and a commitment to accessible, high-quality Islamic education.

He serves as Ustad-ul-Tafseer wa Hadith at Darul Uloom Farooqia, Rawalpindi, and has served as visiting faculty at IIUI, FAST-NUCES, and Fatima Jinnah Women University (FJWU). His academic and teaching experience informs the academy's commitment to scholarly integrity, intellectual depth, and a structured approach to Islamic learning.

Under his leadership, Al-Falah Islamic Academy seeks to make authentic Quranic and Islamic education accessible to students across geographical boundaries, connecting the enduring heritage of Islamic scholarship with the opportunities of modern education.

His vision is to cultivate a learning community grounded in sound knowledge, strong character, spiritual development, and a lifelong commitment to beneficial learning.
