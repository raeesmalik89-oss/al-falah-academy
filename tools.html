How to Start Teaching Online — Step by Step
  1. Install Zoom or open Google Meet — share the link with your student via WhatsApp
  2. Open Excalidraw in your browser — share the link so both of you can write on the same whiteboard
  3. Enable Screen Share in Zoom/Meet to show Quran PDFs, Tajweed charts, or lesson notes
  4. Use Google Classroom to assign homework, track progress, and share recordings
  5. Record sessions with OBS Studio and upload to a private YouTube link for students to revise
