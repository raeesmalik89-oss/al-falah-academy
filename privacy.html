Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Al-Falah Islamic Academy ("we", "us", "our") operates the website alfalah-academy.com and its student/teacher portal. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us - when you fill in the enrollment or contact form, or use the portal, we may collect your name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, the course you are interested in, and any message you send us. Students and teachers who use the portal also have an account (name, login email and role).

Information collected automatically - like most websites, we collect basic, non-identifying usage data such as pages visited, approximate location (country/city), device type and browser, using cookies and analytics. This does not tell us who you are personally.

2. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies: essential cookies that keep you signed in to the portal, and analytics cookies (Google Analytics) that help us understand how the site is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the portal login requires its essential cookies to work.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and enroll you in classes;
  • To run the student and teacher portal (attendance, progress, announcements, live classes);
  • To arrange fees where applicable;
  • To improve our website, courses and service.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Services we rely on

We use trusted third-party services to run the site. Your data may be processed by them only to provide these functions:

  • Google Analytics - website usage statistics;
  • Supabase - secure database and login for the portal;
  • FormSubmit - delivering enrollment/contact form messages to our email;
  • Cloudflare - website hosting and security;
  • Jitsi Meet - live online classes (video/whiteboard).

If we ever display advertising (e.g. Google AdSense), advertising partners may use cookies to show relevant ads; this section will be updated at that time.

5. Children's privacy

Many of our students are children. Enrollment of a child is arranged by a parent or guardian, and student records (name, class, attendance, progress) are kept only to provide their education and are visible to the academy's teachers and administrators. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without a parent/guardian's involvement. Guardians may contact us at any time to review or remove their child's information.

6. How we protect and keep your data

Portal data is stored securely with access limited to authorised academy staff. We keep personal information only as long as needed to provide our services or as required for our records, and then remove it. No online service can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you (or your child). To do so, contact us using the details below and we will respond as soon as we can.

8. External links

Our site may link to other websites (for example downloads, YouTube or Facebook). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites - please read their own policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

10. Contact us
