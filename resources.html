FAQ ❓

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about learning Quran online with Al-Falah Islamic Academy.

How do I learn Quran online with Al-Falah Islamic Academy?
Enrol for a free trial class, then attend live one-on-one online lessons with a certified scholar at a time that suits you. We teach Seerah, Tafseer, Hifz, Tajweed, Quran reading (Nazra), Fahm-ul-Quran, Selected Ahadith, Arabic and English to students of all ages, anywhere in the world.
Is the first class really free?
Yes. Your first trial class is completely free — no credit card and no commitment. You only continue if you are happy with the teaching.
What courses do you offer?
Seerah, Tafseer, Hifz (memorisation), Tajweed, Quran Recitation (Nazra), Fahm-ul-Quran, Selected Ahadith (HEC-aligned for BS, MPhil & PhD), classical (Quranic) Arabic and English. Every course is live and one-on-one with a dedicated teacher.
Who teaches the classes?
Qualified, certified scholars — including Dr. Muhammad Naeem (Ustad-ul-Hadith & Fiqh at Darul Uloom Farooqia, Rawalpindi). Lessons are taught with authentic knowledge and proper adab.
Are classes suitable for beginners, children and women?
Yes. We teach complete beginners, children, and brothers and sisters of all ages — starting from the Arabic alphabet (Noorani Qaida) all the way to advanced Tafseer, Seerah and Hadith.
Can I join from outside Pakistan?
Absolutely. Our online classes are available worldwide — students join from the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, Europe and the Gulf, all from the comfort of home.
