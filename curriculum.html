🎓 For University Students · HEC-Aligned

Seerah & Hadith Studies

Mapped to the HEC (Higher Education Commission, Pakistan) scheme of studies, on the BS → MPhil → PhD pathway.

🎓 HEC curriculum alignment: Content and reading lists follow the HEC-recommended Islamic Studies / Uloom-ul-Islamia & Uloom-ul-Hadith syllabi and standard sources. Level 1 supports BS coursework, Level 2 MPhil, and Level 3 PhD research — including synopsis and thesis guidance.

🕌 Seerah-tun-Nabi ﷺ

Level 1 · BS

Foundational Seerah

Undergraduate — the complete life, in order.

  • Arabia before Islam & the Prophet's lineage
  • Birth, childhood & the trustworthy youth (Al-Ameen)
  • Makkan period: revelation, da'wah & patience
  • Hijrah & the Madinan state and society
  • Major ghazwat: Badr, Uhud, Khandaq & Hudaybiyyah
  • Conquest of Makkah & the Farewell Hajj
  • Prophetic character (Seerah & Shama'il) as a model
For BS students · 4–6 months
Level 2 · MPhil

Analytical Seerah

Postgraduate — themes, sources & methodology.

  • Seerah as a source of law, governance & ethics
  • Thematic study: leadership, diplomacy, social reform
  • Critical survey of classical Seerah literature
  • Comparing primary sources & evaluating reports
  • Research methodology in Seerah studies
  • Contemporary application & the Seerah today
For MPhil students · 6–9 months
Level 3 · PhD

Research & Specialisation

Doctoral — original, critical scholarship.

  • Advanced critical analysis of primary Seerah sources
  • Orientalist writings on the Seerah & scholarly responses
  • Specialised thematic & comparative research
  • Academic writing, citation & publishing standards
  • Synopsis, proposal & dissertation guidance
  • Defending findings & contributing new knowledge
For PhD scholars · research-paced

📜 Selected Ahadith

Level 1 · BS

Selected Ahadith & Intro to Uloom-ul-Hadith

Undergraduate — texts, themes & the basics.

  • Curated ahadith from Al-Arba'een an-Nawawiyyah & Riyad as-Saliheen
  • Themes: intention, iman, knowledge, character & ethics
  • Introduction to the sciences of Hadith (Usool-ul-Hadith)
  • Meaning of sahih, hasan & da'eef; the role of the sanad
  • Major Hadith collections & their compilers
  • Applying the Sunnah to student & daily life
For BS students · 4–6 months
Level 2 · MPhil

Advanced Hadith Sciences

Postgraduate — critical study & method.

  • Mustalah-ul-Hadith in depth: categories & defects
  • Ilm-ur-Rijal & Al-Jarh wat-Ta'deel (narrator criticism)
  • Takhrij-ul-Hadith — tracing & grading narrations
  • Schools & methodology of the Muhaddithin
  • Thematic (mawdu'i) study of selected chapters
  • Research methodology in Hadith studies
For MPhil students · 6–9 months
Level 3 · PhD

Research & Specialisation

Doctoral — original scholarship.

  • Critical analysis of chains, texts (matn) & hidden defects (ilal)
  • Comparative study of Hadith & contemporary issues
  • Orientalist critiques of Hadith & scholarly responses
  • Manuscript & source evaluation
  • Academic writing, citation & publishing standards
  • Synopsis, proposal & dissertation guidance
For PhD scholars · research-paced
📖 Understanding the Qur'an

Tafseer & Fahm-ul-Qur'an

From the meanings of the revelation to understanding the Qur'an in its own words.

📖 Tafseer

Core Modules

What You'll Study

Grounded in Ibn Kathir & Ma'ariful Qur'an.

  • Introduction to Uloom al-Qur'an (sciences of the Qur'an)
  • Principles of Tafseer (Usool al-Tafseer)
  • Asbab al-Nuzul — circumstances of revelation
  • Makki & Madani surahs and their themes
  • Line-by-line tafseer of Al-Fatihah & Juz 'Amma
  • Selected passages of Al-Baqarah & Al-Kahf
  • Major themes: Tawheed, guidance, akhlaq
  • Drawing practical lessons for daily life
6–12 months · 1-on-1 or small group

🌙 Fahm-ul-Qur'an

Level 1 · Foundation

First Words of the Qur'an

Understand nearly half the Qur'an by its most-repeated words.

  • The 125 most frequent Qur'anic words
  • Essential grammar: ism, fi'l, harf
  • Pronouns & prepositions in the Qur'an
  • Word-by-word study of Surah Al-Fatihah
  • Word-by-word of short surahs of Juz 'Amma
  • How to use a word-list & mushaf together
3–4 months
Level 2 · Intermediate

Grammar in Context

Verbs, forms and longer passages.

  • Verb tenses & the ten verb forms (abwab)
  • Common Qur'anic sentence patterns
  • Word-by-word of selected longer surahs
  • Growing your active Qur'anic vocabulary
  • Recognising grammar as you recite
  • Connecting words to their tafseer meaning
4–6 months
Level 3 · Advanced

Comprehension & Tadabbur

Read, understand and reflect independently.

  • Applied I'raab & sentence analysis
  • Introduction to Balaghah (eloquence)
  • Reading new pages with direct comprehension
  • Thematic & reflective study (tadabbur)
  • Self-study skills & continued learning
  • Living the message: reflection to action
5–6 months
📗 Core Qur'an Learning

Recitation, Tajweed & Hifz

The foundation every student builds on — reading, perfecting and memorizing the Qur'an.

Beginner → Full Qur'an

📗 Quran Recitation (Nazra)

For complete beginners, including children.

  • Noorani Qaida — from Alif-Ba to joining letters
  • Harakat, tanween & correct pronunciation
  • Reading words, verses & full pages fluently
  • Progressing to recitation of the whole Qur'an
3–6 months · flexible schedule
Rule by Rule

🎙️ Tajweed

Beautiful, accurate recitation.

  • Makharij — the points of articulation
  • Sifaat — the characteristics of letters
  • Rules of noon & meem, madd and waqf
  • Practical application with correction
4–8 months · certificate on completion
Customized Pace

🧠 Hifz (Memorization)

With a dedicated Hafiz teacher.

  • Structured daily memorization plan
  • Systematic revision (sabqi & manzil)
  • Retention techniques for lasting hifz
  • Ijazah support for qualifying students
Daily 1-on-1 · Ijazah available
🗣️ Language Courses

Arabic & English

Two structured levels each — from the alphabet to real reading, writing and conversation.

🇸🇦 Arabic Language

Level 1 · Foundation

Reading & First Words

For complete beginners — no prior Arabic needed.

  • Arabic alphabet: shapes, sounds & correct Makharij
  • Joining letters & the four letter positions
  • Harakat, tanween, sukoon, shaddah & madd
  • Reading full words & short sentences fluently
  • Everyday vocabulary & Islamic greetings
  • Pronouns & simple nominal sentences
  • Numbers 1–100 & days of the week
3–4 months · Level A1
Level 2 · Intermediate

Grammar & Comprehension

Building blocks of sentences & Qur'anic vocabulary.

  • The verbal sentence (الجملة الفعلية)
  • Past, present & command verbs
  • Introduction to I'raab & the three cases
  • Idafah (possessive construction) & prepositions
  • Masculine/feminine & sound/broken plurals
  • Forming, reading & translating sentences
  • Core Qur'anic vocabulary & root words
4–6 months · Level A2–B1

🔤 English Language

Level 1 · Foundation

Basics & Everyday English

Start speaking and reading from day one.

  • Alphabet, phonics & correct pronunciation
  • Nouns, pronouns, articles (a / an / the)
  • Present simple tense & the verb "to be"
  • Everyday & classroom vocabulary
  • Greetings, introductions & polite phrases
  • Building simple, correct sentences
  • Numbers, time, dates & common questions
3–4 months · Beginner
Level 2 · Intermediate

Grammar & Conversation

Speak, write and understand with confidence.

  • Past, future & continuous tenses
  • Prepositions, adjectives & adverbs
  • Asking & answering questions naturally
  • Paragraph & short-essay writing
  • Guided conversation & speaking practice
  • Reading comprehension & common idioms
  • Email & message writing basics
4–6 months · Intermediate
