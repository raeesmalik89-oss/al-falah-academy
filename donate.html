Help Establish an Islamic Institute & Community Centre in Islamabad

Building a centre for Quranic education, Islamic learning and community service.

السلام علیکم ورحمۃ اللہ وبرکاتہ

By the grace of Allah, we have been running a small institute in Rawalpindi since 2010, teaching the Holy Quran and Islamic sciences from a modest premises. Alhamdulillah, with the steady growth in our students year after year, we have now outgrown that space.

The time has come to take the next step — to extend this blessed work into a full-fledged Islamic institute and community centre in Islamabad, Pakistan, dedicated to Quranic education, Islamic learning, character development and community service. To make this possible, we need two things — land and funds: first to acquire a proposed 7-kanal plot in Islamabad, and then, in subsequent phases, to develop the facilities needed to serve our growing student body and the wider community. This is where your generous support comes in.

Our vision is a welcoming centre where students can learn the Holy Quran, develop Islamic knowledge and values, and benefit from educational opportunities in a supportive environment.

How You Can Help

1

Support the Land Acquisition Fund

Contribute through general donations or voluntary Sadaqah towards the proposed purchase of land for the institute. All contributions are handled transparently and used according to the project's disclosed funding policy.

2

Support Eligible Zakat Recipients

Zakat is collected separately and disbursed strictly in accordance with Shariah guidance, under the supervision of qualified scholars — ensuring every contribution reaches its rightful place.

3

Share Our Mission

Help by sharing this initiative with your family, friends, community organisations and anyone interested in supporting Islamic education.

Our commitment to transparency. Before contributing, donors will be able to review the institute's project information, responsible management, registration status (where applicable), verified payment details, fundraising targets and financial reporting arrangements. We will provide updates on fundraising progress, land acquisition and future development. May Allah accept every sincere effort and reward all those who contribute towards beneficial knowledge and the service of the community. آمین.
